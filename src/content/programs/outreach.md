The chapter has worked with the Defining Your Path–Field Trip Program and offered homework help for middle- and high-school-aged refugees through the Refugee Center in Salt Lake City. Outreach has also included What is Math? days and Expanding Your Horizons.

If you are interested in offering your services for outreach, please contact the chapter.
