The 2024 conference brought together students interested in algebraic geometry, commutative algebra, geometric group theory, and number theory. Its goals were to foster community among underrepresented groups in mathematics, introduce possible research areas, and connect participants with role models and possible mentors.

The conference included lecture series, lightning talks, problem sessions, a panel, and time to eat together. It is part of the RTG in Algebra, Geometry and Topology at the University of Utah.
