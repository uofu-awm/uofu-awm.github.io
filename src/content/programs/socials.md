Socials are often co-organized with the SIAM Student Chapter at the U, GSAC, and/or USAC. Past gatherings have included ice skating, game nights, Riemann’s birthday party, and events for Black History Month, Hispanic Heritage Month, LGBT History Month, Native American Heritage Month, and mathematicians with disabilities.
