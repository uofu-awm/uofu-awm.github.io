The annual professor panel gives graduate students a chance to meet potential advisors: five-minute research talks followed by a casual meet-and-greet with snacks.

For undergraduates considering graduate school, the chapter has hosted panels about applying to math graduate school and what life as a math graduate student is like. Past workshops have included LaTeX introductions and sessions on navigating the job market.

The chapter often runs LaTeX workshops with the SIAM Student Chapter. The point is simple: mathematics eventually assumes that people know LaTeX, and a workshop helps make sure nobody is left at a disadvantage for not having learned it yet.
