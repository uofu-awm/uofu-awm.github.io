The series gives students a chance to interact with mathematicians and to think about their own career trajectories. With support from the RTG in Algebra, Geometry and Topology, the chapter has brought an additional pure-math speaker to campus each semester.

Recent visitors have included Julia Pevtsova (University of Washington), Claudia Miller (Syracuse University), Carolyn R. Abbott (Brandeis University), Ami Radunskaya (Pomona College), Melody Chan (Brown University), Matt Menickelly (Argonne National Laboratory), Ellen Eichen (University of Oregon), Blerta Shtylla (Pfizer), and Autumn Kent (University of Wisconsin–Madison).
