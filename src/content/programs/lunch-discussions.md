Past conversations have included women in mathematics in Chile; LGBTQ+ mathematics conferences; Latinx and Hispanic mathematicians; racism in the mathematics profession; gender and job expectations in academia; and what it is actually like to work in mathematics.

The point is not to have the last word. It is to eat lunch together and make room for conversations that are easy to skip when everyone is busy.
