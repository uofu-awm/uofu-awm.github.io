## Calendar Fall 2018

| Date | Time | Place | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| August 28 | 4:00–4:25 p.m. | JWB 335 | Organizational meeting Come hear about the events we are planning for this year! We welcome suggestions and volunteers. Participation in AWM is open to all interested members of our mathematical community. We hope to see you there! |
| September 4 | 11:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m. | JWB 108 | Lunchtime discussion Led by Jenny Kenkel. A casual meeting to foster a sense of community between AWM members. |
| September 13 | 3:15–4:15 p.m. | LCB 219 | Meet the AWM Event Meet the AWM Officers and members of snacks and scavenger hunt icebreakers! This is also the official start our graduate-to-undergraduate student mentoring network. |
| October 2 | 11:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m. | JWB 108 | Lunchtime discussion Led by Jenny Kenkel. A casual meeting to foster a sense of community between AWM members. |
| October 18 | 5:00–6:30 p.m. | LCB Loft | Graduate School panel Come learn about what life is like in math graduate school from current graduate students. Co-hosted by USAC and AWM. |
| November 6 | 11:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m. | LCB 121 | Lunchtime discussion A casual meeting to foster a sense of community between AWM members. |
| November 6 | 4:35–5:35 p.m. | JWB 335 | Professor Panel Looking for a research advisor? Or just curious to hear what research is done in our math department? Come hear six professors/potential advisors from various fields of math explain what interests them most in their research, and meet them over snacks. |
| November 13 | 12:00–2:30 p.m. | LCB 219/LCB 115 | LaTeX Workshop joint with [SIAM student chapter at the U of U](http://www.math.utah.edu/siamsc/index.html) We will have a short introduction to LaTeX. |
| November 29 | 4:00–5:00 p.m. | JFB 102 | Professional Development Panel: Academia This panel is a joint event with GSAC, and the Graduate Teaching Mentors. Members of the hiring committees at SLCC, Westminster College, and the U will answer questions and give advice about the hiring process in academia. |
| December 4 | 4:35–5:35 p.m. | JWB 335 | Special AWM Discussion with Becca Thomases Come learn about Dr. Thomases' career path and experiences as a mathematician. |
| December 5 | 10:00–11:00 a.m. | LCB 121 | Speaker Series: Becca Thomases Research Talk This talk is co-hosted by our Math Biology research group. Title: Microorganism locomotion in viscoelastic fluids. |
| December 7 | 4:00–5:00 p.m. | JFB 102 | Professional Development Panel: Industry This panel is a joint event with GSAC, SIAM, and the Graduate Teaching Mentors. Mathematicians from companies such as Goldman Sachs, Recursion, and others will answer questions and give advice about the hiring process in industry. |
