## Socials

We regularly organize get-togethers to create a community. This is a great chance to meet new people. The socials are often co-organized with other organizations, for example: [SIAM student chapter at the U of U](https://www.math.utah.edu/siamsc/), [GSAC](http://www.math.utah.edu/gsac/) and/or [USAC](http://www.math.utah.edu/undergrad/usac.php).
