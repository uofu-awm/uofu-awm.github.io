## Calendar Spring 2021

| Date | Time | Place | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| February 2 | 4:35pm–5:35pm | [Zoom link](https://utah.zoom.us/j/97053036699) (password: GSACmath) | Meet the AWM/Welcome the new Department Members We would like to welcome the new department members to advertise our activities and encourage them to be involved with the AWM. Everyone is welcome! |
| March 5 | 12pm–1pm | Zoom (will be sent out) | Meet and Greet with MSU Informal meet and greet with Michigan State's AWM chapter to discuss experiences with navigating graduate life, managing stress, challenges we are facing, opportunities, and much more! |
| March 18 | 4pm–5pm | Zoom (will be sent out) | AWM-RTG Speaker Series The speaker is Ila Varma, who is an assistant professor at University of Toronto. Ila's research interests lie in number theory, specifically arithmetic statistics and Galois representations. In this talk, Ila will be sharing her career path, followed by a Q/A session. |
| April 8 | 3pm–4pm | Zoom (will be sent out) | AWM Speaker Series The speaker is Ryan Hynd, who is an associate professor at the University of Pennsylvania. Ryan's research interests lie in partial differential equations (PDEs), specifically PDEs in mathematical models for fluid mechanics, control theory, finance, and with eigenvalue problems. In this talk, Ryan will be sharing his career path, followed by a Q/A session. |
| April 21 | 12:55pm–1:45pm | Zoom (TBD) | Applying to Grad School Workshop Get information about how to prepare to apply for math graduate school. Come with questions! |
