## Lunch Discussion

In the past we have come together to eat lunch and discuss an article on the intersection of mathematics and topics like gender, STEM education, equity, and diversity. Below we document the themes of these discussions.

### Fall 2022

| Date | Description |
| --- | --- |
| November 10, 2022 | Women in Mathematics in Chile We will discuss the article "Promoting Women in Mathematics: Experiences from Chile." The article can be found [here](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/363116464_Promoting_Women_in_Mathematics_Experiences_from_Chile_Notices_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society). |
| October 26, 2022 | LGBTQ+ in Mathematics We will discuss the article "Space for All: The Rise of LGBTQ+ Mathematics Conferences." The article can be found [here](https://www.ams.org/journals/notices/202106/rnoti-p998.pdf). |
| September 22, 2022 | To honor Hispanic Heritage Month, We will discuss the podcast from Lathisma (Latinx and Hispanics in the Mathematical Sciences) which is an organization that showcases the contributions of the Latinx and Hispanic mathematicians. The transcript from the podcast interviewing Erika Camacheo from 2018 can be found [here](https://www.lathisms.org/podcasts). |

### Spring 2021

| Date | Description |
| --- | --- |
| April 15, 2021 | Towards a fully inclusive mathematical profession We will discuss the recent findings of the American Mathematical Society's (AMS) recent report on racism in the AMS and in the mathematics profession. For the lunch discussion, please read the Preface, Executive Summary, and Chapter 1: Charges, Findings, and Recommendations. We will discuss how the report's findings could be implemented in our local mathematics community. The report can be found [here](https://www.ams.org/about-us/Towards-a-Fully-Inclusive-Mathematics-Profession.pdf). |
