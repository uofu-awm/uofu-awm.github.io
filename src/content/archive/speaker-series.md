## Speaker Series

Once/twice a semester, we invite and host a mathematician from another university to give a technical research talk and a more general talk about their journey in mathematics. The aim of this series is to give students the chance to interact with mathematicians and motivate them in their career trajectories.

As part of the [RTG: Algebra, Geometry and Topology](http://www.math.utah.edu/agtrtg2019/) funded by the NSF award #1246989, we have funds to invite one more speaker in pure math every semester.
