## Mentoring

The University of Utah AWM mentoring network connects undergraduate students majoring, minoring, or interested in mathematics with a graduate student mentor. The program aims to improve community within the math department and provide support to our undergraduate students. Undergraduate and graduate students of any gender are welcome.

The deadline to sign up for the mentorship program has passed. If you've been assigned a mentor or mentee, we ask that you fill out this [form](https://forms.gle/aqLo165bCse2WtTz6) to certify your commitment to the program. We will also be holding a virtual kick-off event on October 1. Any questions should be directed to [Samantha Linn](mailto:linn@math.utah.edu). If you have any concerns, you can submit them [here](https://forms.gle/GhZ7Ng8DVdUHfprA6) with the option to remain anonymous if you would like.
