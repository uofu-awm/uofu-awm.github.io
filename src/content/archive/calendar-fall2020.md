Picture A Scientist is a film based on the personal stories of 3 female scientists and documents the systemic discrimination faced by women in STEM fields. To learn more about the film, you can visit the film's website [here](https://www.pictureascientist.com/). This event is in collaboration with GWBE (Graduate Women in Biomedical Engineering), Curie Club, GALS (Graduate and Advanced Ladies in Science), GIG (Genetic Interest Group), and UCC (University of Utah Counseling Center). All are welcome to participate! Please register for the event by October 9th at 11:59pm. Those who register will be given a private screening link that will be available for the viewer to watch within a 48 hour time frame. |

| October 15 | 6:00–7:00pm | [Zoom link](https://lms-utah.zoom.us/j/8675278568) | Allyship Follow-up discussion from Picture A Scientist, moderated by Macaulie Casey, graduate student, Genetics Interest Group and Rebecca Zitnay, graduate student, Graduate Women in Biomedical Engineering. |
