# Marks and archival-source notes

## University of Utah mark and colors

`public/images/university-of-utah-logo.svg` is the University of Utah logo published by the [University Brand site](https://brand.utah.edu/). It is used without alteration and links to `utah.edu` in the site header.

The site uses the University’s official digital Utah Red: `#BE0000`. The primary University color guidance is available from the [University Brand color guidelines](https://brand.utah.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/69/2023/10/toolkit-uu-color-guidelines-1023.pdf).

## AWM mark

`public/images/awm-student-chapter-logo.jpg` is the official full-color AWM Student Chapters logo published by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](https://awm-math.org/programs/student-chapters-info/student-chapter-logo-acceptable-usage/). It is used without alteration and links to `awm-math.org` in the site header.

## Chapter archive

The Markdown files in `src/content/archive/` are an archival migration of the public pages at `https://www.math.utah.edu/awmchapter/`. Each file identifies its original source URL in frontmatter. Visible source wording is retained verbatim. Page structure and formatting were changed, damaged character encoding and duplicated MathML transcription were repaired, and legacy page links were routed to the local archive.

Public images and documents recovered from the legacy site are stored under `public/archive-media/` and remain attributable to their original authors and publishers. Files missing from the live server were recovered from public Internet Archive snapshots where available. The Materials-page thumbnails could not be recovered, so the local thumbnails are previews rendered from the corresponding original public PDFs.

Six old flyer PDFs (`2013_Origami.pdf`, `2014_MegaMengerPoster.pdf`, `2014_Snowflakes.pdf`, `2015_STEMCareers.pdf`, `2015_StringArt.pdf`, and `2020_LaTeX.pdf`) and three images (`AWM_2024_Officers.png`, `AWM_Blue_Shirt`, and `AWM_Maroon_Shirt.png`) were no longer available from either the legacy server or public web snapshots. Their visible labels or captions are preserved without broken links.
